What is Continuity of Care?

Continuity of care benefits may allow you to temporarily continue certain ongoing care with a provider who is no longer in your AZ Blue network.

Check Your Eligibility

Use this tool to see if your situation may qualify for continuity of care and learn the next steps.

Important: This tool is not a prior authorization request and does not guarantee continuity-of-care approval, coverage, or payment. Your benefits and eligibility will be reviewed after you submit the requested information.
