What This Is

Three jobs, one tool

azblue.com exists in English, Spanish and Chinese. Keeping those in sync by hand doesn't scale — pages get translated late, sections quietly go missing, links break, and content ages out without anyone noticing.

This tool checks every page against its English version and tells you what's wrong. Then it gives you two ways to work through it: a dashboard for the full picture, and a side panel that sits beside the website and shows problems for whatever page you're looking at.

It also handles big content releases. The AEP Launch tab compares the site before and after a deploy, so you can prove every page that was supposed to update actually did.

It installs into Chrome. Nothing is published on the web, and only people who install it can use it.

What It Checks

The problems it finds

Every audit run compares each page against its English version and flags anything that doesn't line up.

Missing translations

A page exists in English but the Spanish or Chinese version is missing or won't load.

Content that doesn't match

The translation has fewer headings, links or images than the English page — usually a sign a section was dropped.

Broken links

Links on the page that lead nowhere, including links to PDFs and forms.

Stale content

Pages that haven't been touched in a long time, so you can review what's aged out.

Untranslated text

English copy or placeholder text left behind on a page that should be fully translated.

Pages that stopped working

Links in the sitemap that now return an error, checked automatically twice a day.

Where You Work

Two views of the same information

Use whichever fits what you're doing. Marking something fixed in one place updates the other — it's one shared list, not two.

The dashboard

The full picture. Every finding across the site, which pages have the most problems, translation coverage by language, and the AEP Launch tools. This is where you run an audit and work through results in bulk.

The side panel

Docks beside azblue.com while you browse. Shows problems for the page you're actually looking at, and highlights them right on the page so you can see what it means.

Sharing Results

Getting findings to the rest of the team

Everyone sees the same list

Findings and progress are stored centrally. When you mark something fixed, your colleagues see that too — there's no exporting and emailing a spreadsheet around to stay in sync.

Power BI

Audit results feed a Power BI report as four tables: findings, broken pages, translation coverage, and stale content. Your Power BI person can connect once and it refreshes on a schedule. Step-by-step instructions live inside the tool, on the Power BI Import Instructions page.

Teams alerts

The site's sitemap is re-checked twice a day. If pages start returning errors, a single message posts to your Teams channel listing what broke — one message per check, not one per page.
