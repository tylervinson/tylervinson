Working Through Them

What to do with a finding

Not everything needs fixing

Some findings are genuinely wrong for your site — a page that's intentionally English-only, or a heading count that differs for a good reason. Mark those Ignore and they stop cluttering the list for everyone.

Marking something done

Once you've made the fix in the CMS, mark it Done. It stays marked until the next audit run confirms it, and your teammates see it immediately so nobody duplicates the work.

Where to start when there's a lot

The dashboard's Top problem pages list ranks pages by how many findings they have. Fixing one page there often clears several findings at once, which is usually faster than working down a flat list.

Severity, in plain terms

Critical means a page or translation is missing or broken — a customer hits a dead end. Warning means the content doesn't match the English version and probably lost something. Info is worth knowing but rarely urgent.

Coming back to the dashboard
The panel's footer has a Full dashboard button — it focuses the existing dashboard tab (or opens one) without closing the panel. The panel stays docked as you switch back and forth.
